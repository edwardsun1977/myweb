電信Network & Telecom

Internet infrastructure grows by leaps every year, and the volume of network and telecom documentation grows with it. Language service providers feel the strain first: they must scale capacity to match their clients' business, while spreading a limited pool of qualified experts across every translation team they run.

We solve that problem structurally. Think of a project team as a cluster rather than a queue: every translator is a node, and the project as a whole is what the client buys. No node holds the glossary, and no node is a single point of failure.

A Cluster, Not a Queue

All translators on your project share a single translation memory in real time over a local network. That hub-and-spoke arrangement keeps glossaries and style consistent — including for translators who join the project halfway through, which is precisely where consistency usually breaks down. It also collapses the many-to-many scaling problem: adding capacity no longer means multiplying the number of hand-offs between people.

The cluster also monitors itself. If a translator becomes unavailable for any reason, the active workload is automatically reassigned across the remaining nodes, and delivery dates hold. In engineering terms, the setup gives you fault tolerance and dynamic load balancing; in commercial terms, it means a translator's holiday is not your problem.

End to End, Without the Queue

Our flat structure connects you directly to the people doing the work. Questions route straight to the translators who can answer them, and answers come back quickly and at low cost — no account layer to relay them through. On that basis we are glad to help with your last mile into the Chinese market, and with the revenue that follows it.
