團隊People & Alliance

Good people are the whole of a localization project. We invest continuously in training so that our linguists keep refining their craft — yet no single translator can be an expert in every technology that reaches the market.

Linguists and Experts Together

That is why we bring freelance subject-matter experts (SMEs) into the team rather than asking translators to improvise. An SME works in a specific field every day: they know the industry, they understand emerging technology, and they recognise when a sentence is technically wrong even though it reads well.

Combining the two gives you the best of both worlds — a linguist who controls tone, terminology and style, and an expert who controls meaning. The result is a deliverable of higher quality, produced in a shorter turnaround time.
