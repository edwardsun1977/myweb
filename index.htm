Why Clients Choose B&B China

After wading through countless pages of inaccurate, unclear — and sometimes outright dangerous — translations, we founded B&B China with a single mission: to deliver better Chinese localization for technical fields.

We take that mission seriously. We never claim expertise we do not possess, and we never pretend to work in every language. Chinese localization is our craft, and we practice it with discipline.

Most of our translators hold technical backgrounds and hands-on localization experience. Because new products and technologies reach the market faster than anyone can master them all, we engage independent subject-matter experts (SMEs) to work alongside our translation teams — ensuring both technical accuracy and high throughput.

In other words, your translators are also doctors, accountants, automotive technicians and network engineers.
