專家Case Study I: CATERPILLAR Manual Saved by Expert Translators

A CAT 3126B industrial diesel engine arrived with a Chinese installation guide that no technician could follow. The engine was sound; the manual was not. This is how the document was rebuilt.

The Challenge

Unlike a traditional mechanical engine, the 3126B was designed for electronic control. The injection pump, fuel lines and nozzles of an older design were replaced by a unit injector in each cylinder, allowing the engine's electronic control module (ECM) to govern the engine completely. To perform as intended, the engine must be wired and programmed correctly.

That was precisely the difficulty facing Xiangfan Rail Vehicle Factory. The installation guide shipped with the engine became a major obstacle: after wading through pages of inaccurate — sometimes dangerous — translation, technicians found the instructions ran counter to their own professional judgement. Eventually the factory came to us for help.

The Solution

We had hands-on experience with Cummins engines, so we began by working alongside the factory's technicians rather than at a distance from them. Understanding how the ECM actually behaves was the key to reading — and then rewriting — the text.

The ECM does not simply switch a pump. It operates through HEUI injectors — hydraulically actuated, electronically controlled unit injectors. In practice the module monitors oil pressure continuously and treats that pressure as the signal on which subsequent solenoid-timed fuel injection depends. Once that relationship was clear, our follow-up rewrite produced a manual that was technically meaningful instead of merely grammatical.

The Result

Recognising the difference, the factory asked us to support the tests that followed: the CAT 970F transmission with its 3Q-6242 solenoid valve group, and the microprocessor-based railcar controller. In the end the whole power train was capable of reversing protection, automatic gear shifting and overspeed prevention.

A translation that contradicts the machine is worse than no translation at all. Our job was to make the document agree with the engine.
