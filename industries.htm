行業Your Industries

In professional translation there is one simple principle, and it holds without exception: if you do not understand, you cannot translate.

Our translators do hold technical backgrounds in the fields they serve. Yet new products and technologies reach the market far faster than any one person can master them all. So we engage freelance subject-matter experts (SMEs) to work alongside each translation team. Because these experts spend their working lives in a single industry, they know its vocabulary, its conventions and its emerging technologies from the inside.

Before a project begins, the assigned SME briefs and trains your translation team until the underlying concepts are genuinely understood. Throughout the translation phase, the SME stays available to answer every question the team raises. The result is the best of both worlds: subject knowledge and linguistic craft in the same delivery, with higher quality and a shorter turnaround.

Alliances That Keep Us Honest

Understanding is not something a translator can claim on a résumé; it has to be tested in working conditions. We have therefore built long-term, mutually beneficial relationships with Hualu Audit Firm (ranked 17th nationally in 2004) and with Xiangfan Rail Vehicle Factory. Those partnerships put our output in front of the people who use it — auditors and engineers — and confirm our expertise in the audit and mechanical fields in practice rather than in theory.

One Partner Across the Whole Deliverable

Industry knowledge is only half the work. A single product may need interface strings, help files, printed manuals and a Chinese website that all read as one voice. We cover that span in-house — software UI and help files, technical documentation, website localization and multilingual DTP — so terminology, style and layout stay consistent from the first screen to the last printed page.
