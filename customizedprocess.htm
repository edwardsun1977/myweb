流程Customized Process Ensures Quality and Timing

Case study: Open Breast Array Coil for the G.E. Signa system — a customized process that delivered technical accuracy and high output at the same time.

The Challenge

When the project came through our door, we wanted to bring our customer the best of both worlds: the technical accuracy contributed by our subject-matter experts (SMEs), and the high output and lighter management burden safeguarded by our in-house translators. In short, with our pool of SMEs available, the question was simply how best to leverage professional ability across the translation team.

The Solution

The selected SMEs had served the medical-equipment industry for years: they knew the product well and understood the technology deeply. At the start of the project, they trained our translation team on the product and the technology behind it. Although our translators already had a general idea of how the fat-suppression system works, the SMEs still demonstrated a precise picture of the null point at which no longitudinal magnetization is applied to fat.

More importantly, during the translation phase the SMEs remained on call to answer questions — freeing our customer from having to field inquiries. With the aid of our web-enabled terminology management tools and translation memory, we leveraged professional ability across the whole translation team, which significantly contributed to both quality and reduced turnaround time.

Expert knowledge where it counts, translated by a team that scales — that is a customized process.
