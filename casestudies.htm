Localization is a regulated subject. You can certainly produce a translation without understanding translation technology, production workflow or the subject matter — but doing so creates real risk for the people who must operate, install or audit the result.

Before you choose a language vendor, take a few minutes to examine what we have delivered under real conditions. Each case below describes a genuine project, the problem we were handed, and what changed once the work was done properly.

What the Cases Have in Common

None of these projects was rescued by vocabulary alone. In every instance the difference came from someone who understood the equipment, the standard or the accounting framework behind the words — and who was given the time and authority to challenge the draft.

Accuracy in a technical field is not a matter of style. A wrong term in an installation guide is an accident waiting to happen.

If your project involves machinery, finance, medicine, networks or regulated documents, we would be glad to walk you through the comparable work we have done. E-mail us with a sample file and we will tell you honestly what it will take.
