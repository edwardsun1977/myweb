What to Send Us

To receive a quotation the same day, include as much of the following as you can:

  • The source files, or a representative sample of a few hundred words.
  • The target language variant — Simplified or Traditional Chinese.
  • Your deadline and the format you need delivered.
  • Any glossary, style guide or previously translated material.

If you are unsure which service fits, describe the document and we will tell you what it needs — including when it needs nothing more than a light edit.
