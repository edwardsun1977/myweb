What Changes in Chinese Typesetting

Setting Chinese is not a matter of swapping fonts. Line breaking, punctuation width, character spacing, justification and the alignment of Latin product names inside Chinese paragraphs all follow different rules — and illustrations often need new labels.

  • Typography — correct use of full-width punctuation, forbidden line-start and line-end characters, and balanced justification.
  • Layout adjustment — columns, captions, tables and callouts reflowed so the Chinese edition reads as the original was designed for it.
  • Artwork — labels, screenshots and diagrams re-lettered in Chinese, with the source art preserved.
  • Prepress — fonts embedded or outlined, colour and bleed checked, print-ready PDF supplied on request.
