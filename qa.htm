質檢QA Sourcing

Having waded through countless pages of inaccurate, unclear — and sometimes outright dangerous — translations, we know deeply how much a qualified QA process matters.

If you absolutely cannot afford to be misunderstood, you need to be confident that the language experts you work with have correct knowledge of the subject area and a proven record of communicating their clients' messages effectively in Chinese culture.

That is why we have developed our own quality-control standard for technical translation. Although it is somewhat empirical, it really works in real conditions — a comprehensive quality rating built on weighted scores across accuracy, terminology, style and cultural fit.

Wrong wording in a manual, a contract or a medical report is never just a typo. QA sourcing exists so it never reaches your users.
