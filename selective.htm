精選Selective Translation

Sometimes you only need the most vital information from a document — and a full translation would simply take too long. That is when our selective translation service is the answer.

Selective translation is tailored to your unique request. Typical selections include:

  • Abstracts and executive summaries
  • Tables of contents
  • Lead paragraphs
  • Headings and section overviews
  • Patent claims
  • Identification of terms of key interest to you

You glean the essence of the document while saving time and money — and if a full translation is later required, simply contact your project manager for an upgrade.
