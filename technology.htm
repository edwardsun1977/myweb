技術Technology

Nothing replaces the skill of a human translator. Used properly, however, translation technology lowers your cost and raises the consistency of the finished text. We apply four disciplines to every project.

Translation Memory

Getting the most from a translation budget matters. If previous work can suggest the wording for today's file, you pay once for a sentence and benefit from it again and again.

Our memory systems are shared in real time across the team over a local network. That guarantees consistent glossaries and style between translators, between components of a project, and between separate projects. We work in Trados, SDLX and Transit.

Localization Engineering

Engineering tools address both the cost and the volume of translation. They protect file integrity, which in turn reduces testing and re-engineering effort, and they extract the maximum reuse from everything translated before.

Terminology Management

Quality translation begins with the right words. Before production starts we review the project files and any material you have already had translated, looking for recurring company-specific terminology. Those terms go into a client glossary; once you approve it, every member of the team works from it. Content and terminology then stay consistent across all files — essential for a quality deliverable.

Content Management

Managing thousands of documents is difficult, especially when the information takes many forms. Our content management system keeps your translation under control: content stays current, and translated versions stay in step with the source.

How the Four Work Together

Consistency

A single approved glossary plus shared memories means the same term is never translated two different ways.

Speed

Reuse removes repetitive work, so updates ship in hours rather than weeks.

Integrity

Engineering preserves tags, links and code, so the localized build behaves exactly like the original.

Traceability

Content management records what changed, when, and by whom — useful at audit time.
