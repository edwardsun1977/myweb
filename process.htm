流程Our Localization Process

Increasing productivity while reducing costs is a tough challenge for every organization today. With workflow optimization, we customize repeat tasks so that your translation project becomes faster and cheaper.

Streamlining delivers immediate benefits for follow-up updates as well: terminology, translation memory and QA templates built in one cycle are re-used in the next, bringing the maximum result from every word we translate.

The Workflow, Step by Step

备

1 · Project Preparation & Product Training

We study your product and reference material, and train the translation team on the product before a single word is translated.

译

2 · Translation with Q&A-Reviewed Test

Translation proceeds under a QA1 template, with a Q&A-reviewed test confirming style and terminology at the outset.

汇

3 · Glossary Creation & Client Review

We create a project glossary, submit it for client review, and implement every revision the client requests.

审

4 · Iterative QA Review

A full QC review runs under the QA2 template. Does the translation pass? If not, improvement instructions are recorded in the comments field and the cycle repeats — pass or update, again and again, until it is right.

排

5 · Engineering, Testing & DTP

Engineering and testing run alongside DTP layout, with customer reporting keeping you informed at every stage.

交

6 · Final QA & Delivery

A final QA pass verifies everything before delivery — so what reaches your users is ready to ship.
