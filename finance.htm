金融Finance & Accounting

Many agencies will tell you they handle financial translation. We prefer to put the claim to the test: our work in this field is judged by industry experts, not by ourselves.

To that end, our translator has sat the uniform CPA examination and works from a solid grounding in accounting, auditing, financial and cost management, and taxation. Figures, provisions and disclosures are read the way an accountant reads them, so a translated balance sheet balances and a translated note still says what the auditor meant.

Learning Our Craft from Auditors

Our long-standing relationship with Hualu Audit Firm (ranked 17th nationally in 2004) has shaped the way we work. What we took from that partnership goes beyond technical vocabulary: it is the professional conduct of the audit room — documented review, evidence before assertion, and a clear separation between what is known and what is assumed.

Out of that discipline came our own working chronology for financial translation, and with it a measure of assurance that our output is sound in all material respects. It is now the benchmark for every client engagement. When a financial document reaches us, we handle it with due care, skill and diligence, under absolute confidentiality, and with proper regard for the regulatory framework that governs it.

Reasonable assurance in all material respects — applied to the translation, not only to the numbers.

On the Record

We contribute articles to the Shanghai National Accounting Institute (ESNAI). On request we provide links to those pieces, which are published on the Institute's official site. The arrangement suits our clients: it lets them follow our command of the subject, and the way we write about it, on a continuous basis rather than on our word alone.

Confidentiality as Standard

Financial material rarely travels alone. It arrives with contracts, board papers and supporting schedules — often bound for a Chinese filing, an investor pack or a published annual report. We hold it under strict confidentiality, keep access to the working team only, and can deliver the finished document through document translation or typeset it for print through multilingual DTP.
