網站Website Localization

By its very nature, the web is the fastest and least expensive route to global reach — but only if the site speaks the language of the market it reaches.

A carefully localized website does more than widen your audience. It shows that you are aware of the people who might buy from you, and that you took the trouble to address them properly. Having invested time and money in an effective site, that investment pays off the moment you translate it.

This calls for sensitive and accurate translation of all content into the local language and culture — and for modification of the components that carry meaning beyond words:

  • Changing character encodings
  • Removing culturally inappropriate icons or images
  • Modifying telephone numbers and addresses to local vendors and offices
  • Parameterizing date, time and currency formats

When your website has to run smoothly and read as native Chinese, turn to B&B China. We take source files such as HTML and XML — and many others — streamline your web globalization process and help you achieve simultaneous release.

More importantly, we go beyond literal translation so that the site functions and reads as though a native speaker wrote it. Visitors will never suspect they are reading a translated English site, which strengthens your credibility and builds trust with your target audience.

Two Levels of Service

Depending on your needs and capabilities, we offer two distinct approaches:

微

Chinese Microsite

Your site is abstracted — summarized into the pages that matter — and the resulting content is translated into Chinese.

  • Low, one-time translation cost
  • Makes your company findable in Chinese search engines
  • Ideal for a first presence in the market
企

Corporate Site

Your corporate site is completely localized and translated for use in the market.

  • Corporate identity and message preserved across markets
  • Full product and company detail for customers and partners
  • Registered with local search engines
  • Maintained and updated quickly and cost-effectively

Engineering, Not Just Words

Localization that stops at the text layer breaks as soon as the site is deployed. We test what we deliver: navigation, forms, encoding, page titles, metadata and search behaviour all checked in Chinese before launch.
